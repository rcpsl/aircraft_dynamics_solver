Address
Start it first with python solver_server_dB.py — it listens on port 5051. If it is not running the map falls back to a rough offline estimate and says so; the offline numbers are not the solver's.
Extra time steps
Extra time steps let aircraft wait instead of all crossing at once, which lowers the loudest moment. Measured on this map: 0 extra gave a +6.99 dB peak, 1 extra gave +4.77 dB, and 2 extra changed nothing while tripling the wait. Leave this at 1. Each step costs roughly 2–5× the solve time, and at 3 the solver ran out its whole budget and returned no answer at all — which is why 2 is the maximum here.
Give up aftermin
How long each of the solver's two phases may search. Running out is worse than waiting: the map then shows a plain feasible route that ignores the group's fairness choices, labelled with a warning. Raised from 4 to 10 minutes for that reason — you can always press Stop solving if a wait is not worth it. A whole solve can take up to twice this.
