### Decide before submission

1. **"Slightly worse routing" holds for Proportional only.**
   * Proportional loses 16–17 % where the optimum is known. Above N = 8 it is within 1.4 %
     of the best decibel routing reached, or below it.
   * Utilitarian loses 86–118 %, because its count objective reduces to airborne time.
   * The paper states both. A reader of Table III would refute a claim that the
     Utilitarian loss is slight.
2. **Above the proof frontier the loss is a lower bound.** The brief called it an upper
   bound.
   * The optimum can only lie at or below the best routing reached, so the true loss is
     at least the measured one.
   * Z3 reports −∞ as its lower bound for 7 of the 8 harvested runs, so no optimality gap
     is quoted.
   * The eighth, Utilitarian N = 16 p1, closed its gap at the optimum.
3. **Lane 2 has one or two decibel orderings per size**, because the queue was
   ordering-major (DECISIONS D30) and 21 runs never started. No metric comparison is
   claimed there; the cells are marked *not testable* and drawn hollow.
