## Experiment 2 --- Travel-time cost of each principle

**Utilitarian.** Largest travel cost against the baseline is 36 fleet-hops at N=30 (1.20 per aircraft), against a shortest-path floor of 84 hops. (`fleet|default|utilitarian|1.75`, partial.)
**Proportional.** Largest travel cost against the baseline is 66 fleet-hops at N=30 (2.20 per aircraft), against a shortest-path floor of 84 hops. (`fleet|default|proportional|1.75`, partial.)
**Equal, Sufficientarian, Prioritarian.** Every aircraft flies a shortest path at every fleet size measured: total hops equal the shortest-path floor and equal the baseline's. These principles impose no travel-time cost at any N here; they reshape *where* the flight time is spent, not how much of it there is. On this setup the horizon is $T_{\max} = $ Chebyshev floor $+\,1$, so that is a property of the principle within the horizon it was given, and `screen_log.md` records what a wider horizon does.
