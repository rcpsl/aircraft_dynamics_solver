## Lever 1 — horizon slack

The tool sends `T_max` = Chebyshev floor + 1, which is barely a timestep of room to detour into. If travel time is flat because no condition *can* detour, widening the horizon should separate the conditions that would spend it from the conditions that would not. Unlimited budget, so a zero here means the principle **declined** the room rather than ran out of clock.

## Lever 2 — cap tightness

Horizon slack acts on the two principles carrying an objective. The cap-based principles need the other lever: a cap that actually binds forces traffic off full tiles, and routing around them is the only way. Equal and Sufficientarian only, since the other conditions send the wide cap by construction and have nothing to tighten.
