Measurement options — how we measure fairness

These apply to every fairness principle we try. We can come back and change them.

1  When we say "fair," are we counting communities, or counting people?

Counting people tells the routing engine that flying over a crowded community "costs" more, so flights are pushed toward emptier zones. Counting communities treats every zone the same regardless of population.

Communities

every community counts the same, no matter the population

People

flights avoid the most-populated communities when they can
